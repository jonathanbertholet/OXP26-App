OXP Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026. Applies to the OXP iOS app, an unofficial companion for Odoo Experience.

What OXP stores

OXP works from a catalog bundled on your device. It does not create an account and does not send your data to a server.

  • Talks you save and reminder preferences stay on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Optional local notifications fire only for talks you save.
  • If you tap the campus map’s location control, iOS may show your position on the Brussels Expo map. That location stays on your device.

What OXP does not collect

OXP does not sell personal information, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use analytics SDKs.
